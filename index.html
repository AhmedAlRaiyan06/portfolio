About Me

Hi! I'm Ahmed Al Raiyan, I'm 20 years old, and I'm currently studying IT Architecture. I was born in Bangladesh and moved to Denmark with my family when I was three years old. I've grown up here in Denmark with my family and have spent most of my life here.

I've always had an interest in IT, technology, and everything related to the digital world. However, I never really had the opportunity to explore that interest properly or find out if it was something for me.

After finishing high school, my original plan was to take a gap year, but plans changed and I decided to give IT a chance. That's what led me to study IT Architecture. I'm looking forward to learning more, developing my skills, and seeing where this journey takes me.
